No Paste

Text paste is always blocked. Image paste is allowed unless you turn on the optional setting below.

Clipboard blocking

No Paste decides what to block from clipboard format metadata such as text/plain or image/png. It does not read, store, or transmit clipboard text, images, files, or other pasted contents.

Changes are sent to open pages where Chrome already allows No Paste to run. Chrome's site access, Incognito, file-url, and per-profile controls continue to decide where the extension can operate; reload a page if it was open before the extension was installed or reloaded.
